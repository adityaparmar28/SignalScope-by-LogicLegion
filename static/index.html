🧬 Dual-Branch Fusion Architecture

SignalScope utilizes a dual-pathway feature extractor that simultaneously inspects visible visual geometry and invisible micro-frequency noise residuals.

[INPUT IMAGE: 3x224x224]
├── Branch 1 (Spatial Semantics): EfficientNet-B0 Backbone (1,280 features)
│ └── Captures macro-artifacts: unnatural textures, anatomical glitches, warped lighting.
└── Branch 2 (High-Frequency SRM): Steganalysis Noise Filters (128 features)
└── Extracts micro-noise fingerprints left by GAN and Diffusion latent decoders.
[FEATURE FUSION LAYER: 1,408 features]
└── BatchNorm1d ➔ ReLU ➔ Dropout(0.3) ➔ Binary Classification Head
[TEMPERATURE CALIBRATION & OPERATING POINT: Threshold = 0.50]
└── Produces honest, non-overconfident probability scores to prevent costly false accusations.
